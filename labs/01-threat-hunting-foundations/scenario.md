# Scenario: The Phantom Credentials

At 08:30 UTC on October 3, 2026, the SOC received a low-priority notification regarding an after-hours interactive logon on workstation `WS-FIN-01` attributed to user `CORP\jdoe`. Standard automated antivirus scans returned clean. However, threat intelligence indicates that a regional financial threat group has been targeting remote corporate identities via credential stuffing.

As the Managed Threat Hunter on duty, you suspect that the adversary may have gained unauthorized access to `jdoe`'s endpoint and performed stealthy staging or discovery commands before establishing an external command-and-control connection.
